7. `onboarding_next_tapped`: 1,920 events across 324 users (5.9 events/user)
8. `player_stem_muted`: 1,508 events across 56 users (27.4 events/user)
9. `player_stem_balance_changed`: **1,054 events** across **7 users** (**175.7 events/user**) ⚠️ *TELEMETRY BUG*

#### Telemetry Root-Cause Analysis
Events for pitch (`player_pitch_changed`), tempo (`player_tempo_changed`), and stem balance (`player_stem_balance_changed`) are firing continuously on every pixel movement during slider drag events (`UIControlEventValueChanged`) rather than waiting for touch release (`UIControlEventTouchUpInside`). 

**Impact**: This floods the analytics pipeline, burns through logging quotas, distorts session event metrics (**71.8 events/session**), and risks payload throttling by GA4 APIs.
