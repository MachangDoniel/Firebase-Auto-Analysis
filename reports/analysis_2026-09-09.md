## 8. New Build / TestFlight / Staged Rollout Alert

* **The Issue:** Version **1.3.09** has reached 99.0% user exposure (609/615 active users). 
* **The Problem:** It introduced a critical engagement drop to **30.1%**, down from the **50.0%** baseline on version **1.3.08**.
* **The Cause:** Telemetry shows a massive volume of UI-driven events. This suggests a **slider debouncing bug** in our pitch and tempo controls, where every minor pixel adjustment triggers multiple GA4 network events. This floods our telemetry pipelines and blocks the user interface thread.
