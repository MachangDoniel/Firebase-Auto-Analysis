Analysis of Slider Telemetry Bug

The events player_pitch_changed (208.2 events/user) and player_tempo_changed (133.7 events/user) are flooding the network payload.
* The Cause: The UI events are wired directly to slider progress updates (UISlider.valueChanged or SwiftUI's onChange(of: sliderValue)). Every single pixel of slider movement fires a GA4 hit.
* The Solution: This telemetry bug bloats GA4 quotas, drives up data egress fees, and violates rate limits. The event must be refactored to trigger exclusively on touch release (UIControl.Event.editingDidEnd or equivalent gesture state .ended).
