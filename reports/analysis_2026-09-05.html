Technical Telemetry Bug: Slider Debouncing Failure

The events player_pitch_changed, player_tempo_changed, and player_stem_balance_changed show extremely high event counts per user (between 243 and 321 events per user).

Diagnosis: The sliders for pitch, tempo, and stem balance do not have debouncing implemented. Every individual pixel move triggers an analytical event. This spam of analytical events risks hitting daily GA4 event collection limits, inflates server-side analytics costs, and can degrade app performance on older devices.
