### Top In-App Events & Feature Lookups

* **The UI Slider Debounce Bug:** The event `player_pitch_changed` was fired **1,676 times by only 8 users**, averaging an incredible **239.5 events per user**. Similarly, `player_tempo_changed` was fired 863 times across 10 users (86.3 events per user).
* **Technical Cause:** The audio engine slider UI does not debounce continuous values. Instead of waiting for a user to finish dragging the slider (`onSlideComplete`), the app fires a GA4 event for every single decimal increment. This creates major telemetry noise and should be fixed in the next release.
