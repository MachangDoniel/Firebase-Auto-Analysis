#### Root-Cause Telemetry Architecture Failure:
The extreme event volume for `player_pitch_changed` (11,451 events across only 17 users) indicates that `UISlider` or SwiftUI `Slider` binding callbacks are triggering telemetry events on **every pixel/frame drag movement** rather than on `touchUpInside` / drag completion or using a debouncing operator (e.g., Combine `.debounce(for: .milliseconds(300))`). This pollutes analytics event quotas and skews events-per-session metrics.
