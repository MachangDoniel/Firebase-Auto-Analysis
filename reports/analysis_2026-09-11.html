🐛 Telemetry Bug Spotlight: Slider Unthrottled Events

The events player_pitch_changed, player_stem_balance_changed, and player_tempo_changed account for 12,957 out of 27,280 total recorded events (47.5% of total payload) while representing only 26 unique users.
* Impact: Rapid depletion of GA4 monthly event quotas, increased network data consumption on client devices, battery drain, and skewed analytics models.
* Fix: Wrap slider state listeners in a 300ms Debounce / Throttle Window or dispatch telemetry events solely on UIControlEventTouchUpInside / editingDidEnd.
