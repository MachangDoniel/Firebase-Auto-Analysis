Telemetry & UX Architecture Defects:

  1. Un-Debounced Sliders (player_pitch_changed & player_tempo_changed):
  2. Issue: 10 users generated 7,752 events. Sliders are firing network events on every frame pixel change during drag.
  3. Impact: Artificially inflates Firebase billable event counts, consumes user bandwidth, and throttles network threads.
  4. Fix: Implement a Combine / RxSwift .debounce(for: .milliseconds(500), scheduler: RunLoop.main) operator on all pitch and tempo sliders.
  5. Aggressive In-App Rate Prompt Fatigue (rate_prompt_shown):
  6. Issue: 122 out of 146 active users (83.5%) received an app store review prompt yesterday.
  7. Impact: Prompts are firing immediately upon launch or during onboarding before value realization, leading to negative reviews or prompt exhaustion.
  8. Fix: Restrict SKStoreReviewController invocation to trigger only after a user completes at least 3 successful stem separations.
